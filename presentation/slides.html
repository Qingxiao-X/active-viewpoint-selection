03 — METHOD

Four Policies, One Shared Evaluator

Same scenes, same starting viewpoints, same acceptance rule — only the decision rule for where to move next changes.

Baseline

Random

Uniform over valid actions. No signal used at all — the floor every policy has to clear.

Observable-only

Greedy (heuristic)

No extra renders. Scores moves by frontality and by moving away from the detected glare blob's position.

Oracle-like

Greedy (preview)

Renders every valid neighbor before picking the best observable confidence. Costly: ~10 renders/episode.

Trained

Learned

A small MLP trained on real collected outcomes predicts move quality — no preview renders needed.

Budget: 6 moves/episode · action grid: 5 azimuth × 3 elevation steps

A simple "move away from the detected glare blob" rule is close to optimal with one light source — the learned model never found a case it couldn't. Its real, defensible claim: matching the render-every-neighbor preview baseline's decision quality at ~5× fewer renders (1.9 vs 10 acquisitions/episode).
07 — ALSO TRIED

A Second Light Source (Didn't Help)

To break the "one glare blob" heuristic, we added a second, independently-randomized light. Across two attempts — the first with the light placed where the camera could never actually see it — dual-light scenes still weren't measurably harder than single-light ones, and greedy stayed tied with learned.

  • Scenes with a visible second highlight: success rates statistically indistinguishable from single-light scenes, every policy.
  • Documented, not discarded — full negative result and methodology in results/isaac_heads_v3/README.md.
08 — TAKEAWAYS

What We Can Claim

  • 01Moving the camera measurably beats one fixed guess under glare — +15.3pp, 95% CI excludes zero.
  • 02The benefit concentrates exactly where physics predicts it should: glossy/medium roughness, not matte.
  • 03A simple observable heuristic is a strong baseline here. The learned model hasn't beaten it — only matched the costly preview oracle at a fraction of the render budget.
  • 04Negative results — dual-light, tight-budget — are kept in the repo, not hidden.
